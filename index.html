About the Project

The Smart Blind Stick is designed to improve mobility and independence for visually impaired people.

Ultrasonic Sensor: Detects obstacles in front of the stick.

GPS Module: Provides geographical coordinates when properly connected.

ESP8266: Provides Wi-Fi connectivity for sending data to an online dashboard.

ESP32-CAM: Offers camera functionality for future visual assistance features.
